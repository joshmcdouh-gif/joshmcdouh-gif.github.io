Delete your Jackpot Arena account

Jackpot Arena is developed by BoxcarKits. This page explains how to delete your account and associated data.

Option 1: Delete in the app (instant)

  1. Open Jackpot Arena.
  2. Tap ⚙️ Settings on the home screen.
  3. Tap Delete my data.
  4. Tap Continue, then confirm with Delete everything.

Your account and data are deleted immediately.

Option 2: Request by email

If you no longer have the app installed, email joshmcdouh@gmail.com with the subject “Jackpot Arena account deletion”. Send it from the Google account linked to your game, or include your in-game player name, so we can find your account. We will confirm and delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account (including any linked Google sign-in and its email address)
  • Your coin balance and full coin history
  • All game and bet history (slots, blackjack, poker, roulette, sports bets)
  • Your leaderboard player name and all leaderboard entries

What is kept

Nothing tied to your account is kept. In-app deletion removes the records right away; email requests are completed within 30 days. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Coins

Coins in Jackpot Arena are virtual, have no cash value, and cannot be redeemed or cashed out. Deleted coins are not refunded or transferred.
